# Part 6 — Enterprise Architecture

Zoom out from one system to the portfolio: how large organizations decide, integrate, govern, and pay for technology — and how AI initiatives succeed or die inside that machinery.

**Maturity target:** Level 4 · **Estimated effort:** 5–6 weeks

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Level | What you'll be able to do |
|---|---------|-------|---------------------------|
| 6.1 | Enterprise Architecture Frameworks in Practice | 3 | Use TOGAF/Zachman *concepts* (viewpoints, capability maps, target-state roadmaps) without the ceremony |
| 6.2 | Architecture Views & Documentation | 3 | Produce C4 and 4+1-style views; document for engineers, security, and executives from one model |
| 6.3 | ADRs & Decision Governance | 3 | Run decision-making that scales: ADR practice, RFC processes, disagreement protocols |
| 6.4 | Enterprise Integration Patterns | 4 | Integrate AI into an existing estate: events, messaging, APIs, batch; anti-corruption layers around legacy |
| 6.5 | Security Architecture & Zero Trust | 4 | Place AI systems inside enterprise security architecture: identity, segmentation, data perimeters |
| 6.6 | Identity & Access Management for AI Systems | 4 | Design authn/z end-to-end: users → apps → agents → tools → data, including service identity |
| 6.7 | Data Governance & Knowledge Management | 4 | Establish ownership, quality, catalogs, and lineage for the data AI consumes |
| 6.8 | Legacy Modernization & AI Adoption Strategy | 4 | Sequence AI adoption in a legacy estate; build vs. buy vs. wait; pilot-to-platform paths |
| 6.9 | Architecture Governance: Boards, Reviews & Standards | 4 | Run review boards that help instead of block; define standards and exception processes |
| 6.10 | TCO & the Business Case for AI | 4 | Build a full business case: TCO, value quantification, risk-adjusted ROI, and the pitch |

## Exit criteria

- A target-state architecture + adoption roadmap for a (real or fictional) enterprise
- A business case for one AI initiative, with TCO and risk register, that survives a mock review board
