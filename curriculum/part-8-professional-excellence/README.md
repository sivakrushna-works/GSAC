# Part 8 — Professional Excellence & Career Development

Turning competence into a career. The market rewards architects who can be *found*, *trusted*, and *understood* — this part builds the professional machinery around your technical judgment.

**Maturity target:** Level 4→5 · **Estimated effort:** 3–4 weeks (plus ongoing practice)

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Level | What you'll be able to do |
|---|---------|-------|---------------------------|
| 8.1 | The GenAI Solution Architect Role & Market | 3 | Map role variants (solution/enterprise/platform/principal), seniority ladders, and compensation drivers |
| 8.2 | Building an Architecture Portfolio | 4 | Present projects and case studies as evidence of judgment, not just code |
| 8.3 | Architecture Interviews | 4 | Perform in system-design and architecture loops: whiteboarding method, trade-off narration, handling pushback |
| 8.4 | Technical Writing & Public Speaking | 4 | Write design docs and give talks that change decisions; build a public voice |
| 8.5 | Consulting & Client Engagement Skills | 4 | Scope, price, and de-risk engagements; run discovery; say no profitably |
| 8.6 | Staying Current Without Chasing Frameworks | 4 | Build a filtering system for the AI firehose anchored in timeless concepts |
| 8.7 | Mentoring & Building AI Teams | 5 | Grow engineers into AI practitioners; design team topologies and hiring loops for AI work |
| 8.8 | Operating as a Principal Architect | 5 | Set standards, steward a portfolio, advise executives, and lead an organization through AI adoption |

## Exit criteria

- Public portfolio with two capstone projects (P17–P20) documented to this repo's standards
- One long-form article or recorded talk
- A mock architecture interview passed with a peer or mentor
