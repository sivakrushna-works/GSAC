# Amendment to the Original Project Brief

The file [`original-project-brief.pdf`](original-project-brief.pdf) (formerly `Project- GSAC (GenAI Solution Architect Curriculum).pdf` at the repository root) is this repository's original generation brief — its constitution. It commissioned "a complete learning system that takes a learner from software engineer to enterprise **GenAI** Solution Architect," and fixed the eight-part structure, the mandatory chapter template, the five maturity levels, the case-study and project programs.

**This amendment supersedes the brief's role target.** Per [ADR-0004](../adr/ADR-0004-reposition-to-ai-solution-architect.md) (2026-07-26), the curriculum's target role is the broader **AI Solution Architect**, reached content-first:

- The classical-ML track: chapters 2.9–2.17 (systems design, data & feature platforms, forecasting, ranking/recommenders & anomaly detection, MLOps engineering, perception, experimentation)
- Governance: 6.11 (model risk management & AI regulation) and the MRM/fairness, model-validation, data-quality, and drift checklists
- Patterns: 7.11 (predictive & scoring patterns), completing the pattern language across both lanes
- Practice: case studies CS51–CS56 (plus the repaired CS24/CS30/CS45) and projects P21–P25
- The ROADMAP as a two-lane journey; the repository review that motivated all of it is preserved as the [first-pass review](reviews/2026-07-26-first-pass-review.md)

Everything else in the brief — concepts over frameworks (ADR-0001), the eight-part structure (ADR-0002), the mandatory template (ADR-0003), the quality rules — remains in force and applies to both lanes.

The PDF itself is retained unmodified as a historical record. Where the PDF and this amendment conflict, this amendment governs.
